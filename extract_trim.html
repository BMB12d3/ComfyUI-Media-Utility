Fast Cut is nearly instant and does not recompress, but the start may align to a nearby video keyframe. Exact Cut re-encodes H.264 for much more precise In/Out points. WAV exports uncompressed 48 kHz PCM. The launcher downloads and caches the FFmpeg WebAssembly engine locally on first setup. After that, MP4/WAV processing runs from the local copy and your media stays on your computer. MP4/WAV export must be run through the included local launcher rather than by double-clicking the HTML file.

Viewer: Scroll the mouse wheel over the image to zoom toward the pointer. When zoomed in, click-drag the image to pan. Use Fit 100% to reset the view.

Zoom timeline: The highlighted box on the Whole Clip bar is the exact range shown by the zoomed scrubber. That range stays locked while you scrub. Use Center on Playhead or Playhead → Left when you want to deliberately slide the zoom window to a new part of the clip.
